\section{Perverted Justice}
Perverted Justice was a volunteer organisation whose members posed as children in chat rooms and, after a conviction, published the logs of their conversations with offenders. 

\section{PAN12}
The PAN12 dataset was released for the Internatial Sexual Predator Identifcatio Competiion at PAN-2012. PAN12 contains PJ conversations with a much larger body of non-predatory chat, indlucing secual conversations between adults, so that a classifier cannot simply learn that sexual content means grooming.
